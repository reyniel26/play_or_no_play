Play or No Play?

A simple decision tool to help you choose what's best for you.

Do the right things first. Then enjoy your games — without guilt.

ℹ️

Answer these five questions honestly, not based on how you feel about gaming, but on what's best for your goals and well-being. This is your personal checkpoint, not a judgment of your worth.

1🎯

Have I completed today's most important task, or my minimum acceptable progress on it?

2📋

Have I handled my essential responsibilities?

3🕒

Am I choosing to play during genuine free time?

4🧠

Why do I want to play right now?

5🛑

Have I decided on a specific stopping time?

💡 If You Fail the Test: 15-Minute Reset

  1. 1
    Disconnect. Close the game and put your phone or controller away.
  2. 2
    Ground yourself. Stand up, drink water, and take five slow breaths. Be kind to yourself.
  3. 3
    Choose one task. Write down the smallest meaningful action you can finish.
  4. 4
    Work for 15 minutes. No gaming, social media, or switching tasks during this block.
  5. 5
    Reassess. Continue if needed. If your minimum target is complete and you have time, choose a limited gaming session.

📜 Your Gaming Contract

  • Before gaming: I must complete my minimum daily target.
  • While gaming: I must have a stopping time and honor it.
  • After a setback: I will do one useful thing rather than surrender the rest of my day.
⛰️ Discipline today = Freedom tomorrow. Missing a target doesn't make you a failure. Reset, then choose your next action.
